# About Notiq

**Notiq** is a private note-taking app with voice transcription and on-device speech recognition.

It helps you capture ideas, thoughts, meetings, and tasks effortlessly — just speak or type. Your voice is instantly turned into searchable text, with optional time-coded transcripts for longer recordings. All processing happens directly on your device.

Notiq is built for people who want to stay productive without compromising their privacy.

---

## What you can do with Notiq

- 🎙 Record voice notes and get fast, on-device transcriptions
- ✅ Create to-dos by speaking — your words are turned into actionable tasks
- 🎧 Record long meetings in the background with time-coded transcripts
- 🔎 Search across all notes and tasks in seconds
- 📂 Organize content with folders

---

## Built for privacy

- 🔒 Notes and recordings are encrypted and stored locally on your device
- 📵 No cloud sync and no access to your personal content
- 🧠 AI features run on-device, without sending data to external servers
- 🔐 Optional app-level protection with Face ID or passcode

## Why users choose Notiq

- Works offline
- No account required
- Full control over your data
- Designed for focus and productivity
