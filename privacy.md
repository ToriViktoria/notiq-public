# Privacy Policy for Notiq: Private Notes

**Effective Date**: January 27, 2026

Notiq: Private Notes ("we", "our", or "us") is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your notes, recordings, and transcriptions remain on your device and are never accessed by us.

---

## User Content

- All text notes, audio recordings, transcriptions are stored locally on your device.
- Content is processed using on-device technologies only.
- We do not collect, store, transmit, or analyze your notes, recordings, or transcriptions.
- We have no technical access to your personal content.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

- Audio recordings or voice data
- Text notes or transcriptions
- Personal content of any kind

---

## Limited Non-Content Data

To operate, improve, and market the app, Notiq may collect limited **non-content** data, such as:

- App version and build number  
- Device type and operating system version  
- Country or region (derived from system settings)  
- Anonymous usage events (e.g. feature usage, screen views)  
- Subscription status and purchase events  
- Crash reports and performance diagnostics  

This data is **never linked to your notes or recordings**.

---

## Third-Party SDKs

Notiq uses the following third-party SDKs:

### **RevenueCat**
Used for subscription and purchase management.  
Does not have access to user content.

### **Firebase**
Used for crash reporting, app stability, and basic analytics.  
Does not collect or process user-generated content.

### **Mixpanel**
Used for product analytics to understand feature usage and improve the app experience.  
Events are anonymous and do not include personal content.

### **AppsFlyer**
Used for advertising attribution and campaign performance measurement.  
Does not receive access to notes, recordings, or transcriptions.

### **Meta SDK**
Used for advertising measurement and attribution.  
Meta does not receive user content from the app.

All SDKs are configured to operate without access to user-generated content.

---

## Advertising & Tracking

- Notiq does not sell personal data.
- We do not share notes, recordings, or transcriptions with advertisers.
- Advertising SDKs are used only for attribution and campaign measurement.
- App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is respected. Tracking permissions are requested only when required by the system.

---

## Encryption & Security

- All notes and recordings are encrypted locally using AES-256.
- Encryption keys are protected by the device’s secure hardware and passcode mechanisms.
- Optional app-level protection (Face ID / passcode) is handled entirely on the device.

---

## Data Storage & Backup

- Notiq does not sync data to external servers.
- All user content remains on your device.
- Any backups are controlled by the operating system and user settings.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Notiq does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since all content remains on-device, no personal information is transmitted or stored by us.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features and integrations evolve. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
